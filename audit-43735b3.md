### Genuinely proved material

- The displayed Frey model has
  `Δ = 16 A^8 B^8 (A^4 + B^4)^2`.
- The natural-number discriminant has the same positive-`16` formula.
- The test case `(A,B)=(1,1)` gives discriminant `64`.

They do not compute a Kodaira symbol or the Néron conductor.

- Tate's algorithm for this integral Weierstrass model at `29`;
- a Tate-curve uniformization attached to the displayed Frey curve;
- inversion of the full Tate `j`-series to construct its parameter `q`;
- identification `v₂₉(q)=v₂₉(Δ_min)`;
- Kodaira type `I₈` or `I₀*` and the associated conductor exponent;

- the mod-`13` inertia representation and the implication
  `13 ∣ v₂₉(q) → ρ̄E,13(I₂₉)=1`.

The exact first missing bridge is not the integer divisibility theorem; it is
the curve-to-Tate-parameter/Galois-action interface.

### Missing infrastructure

The pinned library/project lacks:

- an elliptic-curve `L`-function object connecting the rational `L/Ω`

- Kato/Kolyvagin's theorem `L(E,1) ≠ 0 → algebraic rank 0` and finiteness of

  torsion;

  cardinality into Mordell-Weil finiteness;
- a constructed `Fintype (MordellWeilGroup E)`.

The correction from `Subsingleton` to
`Nonempty (Fintype (MordellWeilGroup E))` is mathematically appropriate for
rank zero with nontrivial torsion, but the latter remains uninhabited.

## Dependency order and bridge consequence

The viable dependency order remains:

1. **Tate/local conductor:** construct the local curve, Tate parameter,
   inertia action, and Néron conductor statement.
2. **Mazur/irreducibility:** connect the Frey curve to a genuine
   mod-`13` representation and exclude the reducible/Borel case.
3. **Ribet:** combine modularity, irreducibility, local unramifiedness, and
   the conductor calculation to obtain a residual newform at level `32`.
4. **Kolyvagin:** independently connect analytic or 2-descent input to a
   finite Mordell-Weil group.

At this pin, the proposed bridge

```lean
Δ = 16 A^8 B^8 (A^4 + B^4)^2 → HasNewformAtLevel 32
```

cannot be unconditional. The discriminant identity and its valuation
consequences are proved, but `HasNewformAtLevel 32` additionally needs actual
terms for modularity, Frey-specific irreducibility, the local Néron/inertia
result, and Ribet level lowering. Naming any of those missing conclusions as
another `def Prop`, adding it as an assumption, or declaring an axiom would
not close the proof obligation.

## Audit conclusion

All four branch files are honest about their boundaries: their numerical
display theorems are inhabited, while the substantive literature results
remain transparent `def Prop` placeholders. None of the four principal
foundations obligations is semantically closed by these branches.

The immediate blockers are library-scale formalization gaps, not Lean syntax,
build routing, or arithmetic normalization:

- Tate curves, local conductor and inertia representations;
- modular curves/isogenies and Frey residual irreducibility;
- elliptic-curve modularity and Ribet level lowering;
- elliptic `L`-functions, Kolyvagin/2-descent, and Mordell-Weil finiteness.

No target proposition was added as an assumption or axiom during this audit.
